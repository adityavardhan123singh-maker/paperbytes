Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Notice

This privacy declaration is provided pursuant to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of the Republic of India:

1. In-Browser Client Isolation: 14 of the 15 utility modules process binary data exclusively within your web browser's local sandbox memory (V8/WASM). No data, payload, or image stream is transmitted across the internet to external servers.

2. Ephemeral Cloud Conversion: For the PDF-to-Word module, files are received over secure HTTPS, processed in volatile container memory, returned directly as an attachment stream, and immediately unlinked. No documents are retained, logged, or indexed.

3. Zero Analytics / Profiling: PaperBytes collects no cookies, runs no user tracking scripts, and maintains no database of user identities.
